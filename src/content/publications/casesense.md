CaseSense is an interactive system for exploring a legal case landscape along multiple dimensions at once, rather than as a ranked list of search hits. The interface pairs an exploration panel, where users issue natural-language queries and adjust how much weight each analytical axis carries, with a tri-axial graph view that lays cases out according to those weighted dimensions. Selecting a case opens an information panel with structured extractions from the decision, so that legal researchers can move between the overall map and the details of an individual case.

The work continues the lab's line of LLM-augmented interfaces for legal research that began with [LegisFlow](/publications/legisflow), shifting the focus from statute amendment histories to the structure of case law itself.
